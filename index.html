📚 Online Banking Safety

What is Online Banking?

Online banking allows you to check your account, transfer money, pay bills and manage banking services using the internet.

Basic Safety Rules

  • Use only the official banking app or website.
  • Keep your password private.
  • Never share OTP with anyone.
  • Never share ATM PIN or UPI PIN.
  • Check the receiver before making payments.
  • Keep your mobile and banking applications updated.

Remember

Banks do not normally ask you to share your OTP, ATM PIN, UPI PIN, CVV or password over phone calls or messages.

Important Password Rules

  • ❌ At least 8 characters
  • ❌ One uppercase letter
  • ❌ One lowercase letter
  • ❌ One number
  • ❌ One special character

Never Use

  • Your name as password
  • Your date of birth
  • Mobile number
  • 123456 or password
  • The same password everywhere

🚨 Scam Awareness

Phishing

Phishing messages try to trick you into clicking fake links or giving personal information.

Fake Banking Calls

Fraudsters may pretend to be bank employees and ask for OTP, PIN or other confidential information.

Fake KYC Messages

Be careful with messages saying your account will be blocked unless you complete KYC immediately.

UPI Fraud

Never enter your UPI PIN just to receive money. Always verify payment requests before approving them.

🆘 Emergency Help

If You Suspect Fraud

  1. Contact your bank immediately.
  2. Block your card if required.
  3. Secure your banking account.
  4. Keep transaction details and screenshots.
  5. Report the fraud through the appropriate official channel.

Important

Never share your OTP, ATM PIN, UPI PIN, CVV or password while asking for help.
