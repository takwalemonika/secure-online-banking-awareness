🔐 Password Safety

Why is a Password Important?

Your password helps protect your account from unauthorized access.

❌ Avoid Easy Passwords

❌ password123

❌ 12345678

❌ Your name

❌ Your birthday

💡 Use a Memorable Passphrase

Use several unrelated words that you can remember.

Example: Mango • River • Cloud

🔐 Add Variety

🔠 Uppercase letters

🔡 Lowercase letters

🔢 Numbers

⭐ Special characters

Important Rules 🛡️

🔒 Never share your password.

🔄 Use different passwords for important accounts.

👀 Do not save passwords on public computers.

⚠️ Scam Awareness

📱 OTP Scam

A fraudster may pretend to be a bank employee and ask for your OTP.

Never share your OTP.

☎️ Fake Bank Calls

A caller may threaten that your account will be blocked and ask for confidential information.

🔗 Fake Links

Fraudsters may send fake links through SMS, WhatsApp, or email.

Do not enter banking information on suspicious websites.

🎣 Phishing

Phishing uses fake messages or websites to steal personal information.

🚨 Emergency Help

Think You Have Been Scammed?

Act quickly and stay calm.

1️⃣ Contact Your Bank

Contact your bank using its official customer support channel.

2️⃣ Stop Sharing Information

Do not share any more information with the suspected fraudster.

3️⃣ Secure Your Accounts

Change passwords if you believe your account information was exposed.

4️⃣ Report the Incident

Report the incident through appropriate official banking and cybercrime channels.
