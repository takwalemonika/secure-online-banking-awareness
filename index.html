🔐 Password Safety

Why is a Password Important?

Your password protects your bank account from unauthorized access.

Avoid Easy Passwords ❌

❌ password123

❌ 12345678

❌ Your name

❌ Your birthday

Use Memorable Words 💡

Use several unrelated words that are easy for you to remember.

⚠️ Scam Awareness

📱 OTP Scam

Fraudsters may pretend to be bank employees and ask for your OTP.

Never share your OTP.

☎️ Fake Bank Calls

Unknown callers may ask for confidential banking information.

🔗 Fake Links

Do not click suspicious links received through SMS, WhatsApp, or email.
